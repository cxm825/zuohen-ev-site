FAQ

Compliance questions
we get asked most.

Are your chargers certified for the European market?

Our AC and DC charging equipment is supplied with CE marking for EU and EEA markets. The applicable directives and the declaration for your exact model are confirmed during quotation, since scope varies by power rating and configuration.

Can you provide UL documentation for North America?

UL 2251 compliance applies to our EV connectors and couplers, and FCC compliance covers equipment with communication modules. We confirm which documents apply to your specific model and destination market.

Do you support third-party testing before mass production?

Yes. We can supply sample units for independent TÜV or equivalent testing, and we will work with your test house on the documentation they need. This is common for distributor and fleet projects.

What IP rating should I specify?

It depends on the installation environment. Indoor home and workplace wallboxes are typically IP54, while outdoor DC stations and connectors are commonly specified between IP55 and IP67. We will confirm the exact rating per model.

Can I review certificates before placing an order?

Yes — send us the product category, target power and destination market through the inquiry form, and we will return the matching certification documents for your review.
