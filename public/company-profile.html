QUALITY CONTROL

4-stage verification
before anything ships.

Every production run passes four checkpoints. Non-conforming material is stopped at the stage where it fails, not discovered at the customer's dock.

Incoming raw material QC

100% spectral analysis on ABS and polycarbonate resins and incoming electrical components, so material grade is proven before it enters production.

In-process CMM inspection

Continuous 3D coordinate measuring machine testing against OE tolerances, catching dimensional drift while the batch is still running.

Environmental stress testing

96-hour salt-spray corrosion testing, thermal shock cycling from −40 °C to +85 °C, and IP67 waterproof chamber verification.

Pre-shipment optical check

Final fitment inspection, custom laser engraving verification and shockproof export packaging confirmation before release.
