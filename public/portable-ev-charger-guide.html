Procurement checklist

Start with the operating scenario, target power and connector standard. Then confirm communication, protection, cable length, certifications, packaging and support requirements with engineering.

  • Define vehicle and charger compatibility.
  • Separate confirmed information from optional configuration.
  • Request drawings, compliance documents and samples when needed.
  • Plan after-sales support for the destination market.

FAQ

Common sourcing questions.

Can ZUOHEN customize this product range?

OEM/ODM options may include branding, housing, cable, connector, labels, packaging and documentation, subject to model selection.

Are all technical parameters fixed?

No. Some values are model-specific or configuration-dependent. Confirm final electrical, mechanical and compliance details with engineering.

How can I request a quotation?

Use the inquiry form with your target market, quantity, power, connector and project requirements.
