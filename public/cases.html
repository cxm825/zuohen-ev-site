HOW PROJECTS RUN

From first inquiry
to repeat order.

The same sequence applies whether you are ordering samples or planning a multi-container project.

STEP 01

Requirements review

You share target market, vehicle standards, power range, quantity and timeline. We identify which equipment classes and connector configurations actually fit — and flag anything that will not pass local compliance.

STEP 02

Recommendation & quotation

We return a shortlist of models with specifications, applicable certifications, MOQ, lead time and pricing. Samples can be arranged for validation before committing to volume.

STEP 03

Customization & documentation

For OEM/ODM programs we finalize branding, housing, cable lengths, interfaces and packaging artwork, and prepare the compliance documents your market requires.

STEP 04

Production & inspection

Your order runs through the four-stage verification process. We can support third-party inspection and provide test documentation alongside production traceability.

STEP 05

Export & after-sales

Equipment ships in shockproof export packaging with documentation for customs. After delivery we support technical questions and spare parts to protect your customers' uptime.
