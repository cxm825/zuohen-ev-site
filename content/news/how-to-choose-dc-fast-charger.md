## Buyer sourcing guide

WHAT BUYERS SHOULD KNOW Source with clarity. Configure with confidence. For choosing a DC fast charger, the right specification depends on the destination market, vehicle fleet, electrical supply, installation environment and required user experience. ZOHEN presents this guide as an independent sourcing reference for distributors, installers and project teams. Browse related products → Procurement checklist Start with the operating scenario, target power and connector standard. Then confirm communication, protection, cable length, certifications, packaging and support requirements with engineering. Define vehicle and charger compatibility. Separate confirmed information from optional configuration. Request drawings, compliance documents and samples when needed. Plan after-sales support for the destination market.
